Purchase and Sale Agreement

Standard Purchase and Sale Agreement - Real Property

NOTICE: This is a legal and binding Agreement for the purchase and sale of property. It is appropriate for most, but not all, such transactions. If this form does not appear to either Buyer or Seller to be appropriate for a particular transaction, you are urged to discuss the purchase or sale with an attorney BEFORE YOU SIGN. Most, but not all, provisions of this Agreement are subject to negotiation prior to execution.

1. Parties

This Agreement to buy and sell real property (the "Agreement") is made between the following parties:

Seller agrees to sell and Buyer agrees to buy, for the purchase price and upon the terms and conditions stated herein, the real property together with all buildings and other improvements thereon and all appurtenances thereto, in the same condition as they were on the date of Buyer's signature, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

Unless mentioned below, all personal property is excluded.

5. Deposits

The Deposit(s) specified above shall be made at the stated times. All Deposits shall be made to the Listing Broker or the attorney/title or escrow company conducting the closing. Except at the time of closing, when the deposit shall be delivered to Seller or Seller's designee, the Listing Broker shall not pay the Deposit to anyone without the written consent of all parties to this Agreement. In the event any deposit funds payable pursuant to this Agreement are not paid by Buyer, Seller may give written notice of such failure to Buyer. If such notice is given and a period of five (5) business days passes without Buyer paying the Deposit owed, Seller may declare Buyer in default and shall have the remedies set forth in the "Buyer's Default/Seller's Default" paragraph.

Deposit funds shall be held by the designated title company, closing attorney, escrow agent, or other agreed neutral escrow holder and shall be applied at closing or disbursed according to this Agreement and applicable law. Unless otherwise stated in writing, Buyer shall not be required to increase the deposit after the initial deposit has been delivered.

6. Condition of Premises

Buyer represents that Buyer has examined the property and is satisfied with the physical condition, subject to the Inspection Contingency, if applicable. Neither Seller nor any representative of the Seller or Buyer has made any representation or promise other than those expressly stated herein, which Buyer has relied upon in making this Agreement.

7. Inspection Contingency

a. Buyer shall have the right to inspect all aspects of the Property, including, but not limited to, the physical condition of the property, the value of the property, the title, the encumbrances, and any matters not shown by public record, and shall have the right, up to the time of Closing, to terminate this Agreement for any reason, in Buyer's sole discretion. Seller is expected to have the utilities on during the time of inspections, and to turn them on if the utilities are currently off. If Buyer is not satisfied with the physical condition of the real property, and so notifies Seller in writing prior to the Closing Date, then Buyer may, at Buyer's option, terminate this Agreement. Buyer may give Seller the option to correct the conditions that are unsatisfactory to Buyer. Should Buyer elect to terminate this Agreement, or should Seller be unwilling to correct any unsatisfactory conditions, Buyer shall notify Seller before the time of Closing of Buyer's election to terminate this Agreement, and if terminated, this Agreement shall be null and void and any deposit monies paid hereunder shall be returned immediately to Buyer, and neither Buyer nor Seller shall have any claims against each other under the terms of this Agreement. If Buyer fails to notify Seller as provided herein, this contingency shall be deemed satisfied and this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.

8. Access

Within one (1) business day of the Effective Date, Seller agrees to provide Buyer and Buyer's authorized representatives, contractors, inspectors, appraisers, partners, prospective assignees, and/or end buyers reasonable access to the Property for inspections, due diligence, photographs, measurements, contractor estimates, showings, and other activities reasonably necessary to evaluate, market, assign, finance, or complete the purchase. If the Property is listed with a real estate broker or agent, Seller may coordinate access through the listing agent or another reasonable method, including scheduled or supervised showings. Seller shall use reasonable efforts to maintain access through closing. Buyer agrees to comply with reasonable access procedures established by Seller or Seller's agent and shall be responsible for damage caused by Buyer's negligence or willful misconduct.

9. Lead-Based Paint

The parties acknowledge that dwelling units constructed prior to 1978 are likely to contain lead-based paint, which could create a health hazard. In the event that the real property which is the subject of this Agreement consists of or contains a residential unit built prior to 1978, the parties agree that each party has received, reviewed, signed, and annexed hereto a completed Disclosure and Acknowledgment Form regarding Lead-Based Paint, as required by federal HUD/EPA disclosure regulations.

10. Possession / Delays / Closing Date

Unless otherwise stated herein, Buyer shall receive exclusive possession and occupancy with keys on the Closing Date. The Real Property shall be maintained by Seller until the time of Closing and shall be transferred in broom-clean condition, free of debris and personal belongings. Buyer shall have the right to a walk-through inspection of the Property within 48 hours prior to the Closing Date. Seller agrees that timely performance of its obligations to close on the scheduled Closing Date is an essential term of this Agreement.

11. Warranty Deed

Seller agrees to convey fee simple title of the Real Property to Buyer by a good and sufficient Warranty Deed, subject only to any and all provisions of any ordinance, municipal regulation, public or private law, restrictions, and easements as appearing of record, if any, provided they do not affect the marketability of title. All real estate taxes, utilities, water and sewer charges, and the current water and sewer assessment balance, if any, shall be paid by Seller at closing or, if appropriate, prorated at closing, and shall not be considered an impediment to title. Seller warrants that Seller has no knowledge and/or notice of any outstanding violations from any town, city, or state agency relating to the property.

12. Title Approval

Title to be conveyed by Seller shall be fee simple marketable title ("Marketable Title") and insurable at standard rates by the title company of Buyer's choosing. Seller further agrees to execute such documents as may be reasonably required by Buyer's title insurance company or by Buyer's mortgage lender. Buyer has the right to accept or reject the title commitment from the title company. If Buyer rejects the title commitment for any reason, Buyer may terminate this contract and receive the Earnest Money, thereby releasing Seller from this contract. Upon any title objections that may occur, Buyer has the right to extend this contract for such time as necessary for Seller and the Title Company to clear title, or Buyer may terminate this contract and receive the Earnest Money, thereby releasing Seller from this contract. Buyer has seven (7) days after objections to title have been cured to close, or the aforementioned Closing Date, whichever is later.

13. Adjustments / Prorations

Unless otherwise expressly agreed upon, all fees shall be adjusted pro rata as of the Closing Date in accordance with what is normal and customary in the state where the property resides, including but not limited to, if applicable: real property taxes and assessments, interest, escrow fees, transfer tax fees, association fees, water and sewer usage, and rent. Rent security deposits, if any, shall be credited to Buyer by Seller on the Closing Date and shall include any interest accrued to the tenant.

14. Buyer's Default / Seller's Default

If Buyer fails to comply with any terms of this Agreement by the time set forth for compliance, and Seller is not in default, Seller shall be entitled to all initial and additional deposit funds provided for in Section 4, whether or not Buyer has paid the same, as liquidated damages, and both parties shall be relieved of further liability under this Agreement.

15. Risk of Loss, Damage

All risk of loss or damage to said property, by any means, shall be assumed by Seller until delivery of the Deed, provided Buyer did not cause the loss or damage. In the event the loss or damage exceeds $5,000.00, Buyer shall have the option to receive any insurance payment on account of said damage and take title, or to rescind this Agreement and receive back all deposit money paid. If Buyer rescinds the Agreement, all rights and obligations of the parties under this Agreement shall terminate.

16. Common Interest Community

If the property is a unit in a condominium or other common interest community, Seller will deliver the resale documents in accordance with local statutes and customs.

If Seller fails to deliver such disclosures within the time specified above, Buyer reserves the right to all remedies available to Buyer under applicable law.

19. Assignability and Novation

Seller hereby grants to Buyer, its successors, assigns, nominees, partners, and/or affiliated entities, the irrevocable right to assign, sell, transfer, or otherwise convey Buyer's contractual interest in this Agreement to any third party, including another investor or end buyer, without further consent from Seller. Buyer may also partner with other investors or novate this Agreement with a replacement agreement. Seller acknowledges and agrees that Buyer may market and transfer its contractual interest for a profit. Upon assignment or novation, Buyer may be released from further obligations if the assignee or replacement purchaser assumes the obligations of this Agreement, subject to the closing agent's requirements. Seller agrees to reasonably cooperate with the title/escrow company and execute customary documents necessary to complete the assignment, novation, or direct closing, provided Buyer is not required to incur an obligation to purchase the Property solely because an assignee or end buyer fails to close.

20. Disclosures and Contract Provisions

Seller understands, upon signing this Agreement, that Buyer will possess a contractual interest in the property, and agrees that Buyer, in its sole discretion, has the exclusive right to market the Property and/or this Purchase Agreement for sale to third parties in any way prior to settlement of the Purchase Agreement. Such marketing may include listing the property for sale in a multiple listing service. Seller consents to Buyer conducting such marketing, at Buyer's expense, in an effort to fulfill the obligations outlined within this Purchase Agreement.

21. Resale of Property

Seller agrees that Buyer, on this contract, retains "all profit," whether by note, trade, or cash, in the event of resale of this property, simultaneous close, assignment, or novation of this contract. Buyer may advertise, market, assign, or otherwise transfer Buyer's contractual interest in this Agreement before closing. Any assignment, resale, or other transfer is a transfer of Buyer's contractual rights and does not constitute a representation that Buyer is the owner of the Property before closing.

22. Equal Housing Rights

This Agreement is subject to all local statutory laws prohibiting discrimination in commercial and residential real estate transactions.

25. Email or Fax Transmission

The parties acknowledge that this Agreement and any addenda, modification, and/or notices due hereunder may be transmitted between them by facsimile machine, e-fax, or email, and the parties intend that an emailed or faxed document containing either the original and/or copies of the parties' signatures shall be binding and of full effect.

26. Complete Agreement

Each party acknowledges that this Agreement contains the entire agreement between Buyer and Seller concerning this transaction and supersedes any and all previous agreements concerning the Property, and that such party has not relied on any oral contract, statement, representation, or other promise not expressed in writing in this contract. Any extensions or modifications of this Agreement shall be in writing, signed by the parties.

27. Memorandum of Contract

Seller agrees that Buyer may execute, acknowledge, and record a memorandum of this contract in the official records of the county, at Buyer's discretion and at Buyer's expense.

28. Notice

Any notice required or permitted under the terms of this Agreement by Buyer or Seller shall be in writing, addressed to the party concerned using the address stated in Paragraph 1 of this Agreement, or to such party's attorney, or to the party's Listing Broker or Cooperating Broker.

29. Short Sale

In the case of a short sale, the parties shall work together so that the bank/lender or any lien holders agree to hold Seller, or named persons on title as of this Agreement, harmless from any further action, and will not hold Seller accountable for any remaining amounts owed.

31. Seller's Agreements and Covenants
